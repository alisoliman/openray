One quiet surface for everything you reach for.

A new visual language and interaction model for OpenRay on macOS 26. It covers every step from pressing ⌥ Space to configuring the app. Every feature now lives in one glass panel. Features are scopes you step in and out of, and the keyboard always knows what Return will do.

01 · Principles

Five rules every screen follows

These rules resolve design disagreements. If a proposed screen breaks one of them, the screen changes, not the rule.

01

One surface

Search, act, create and transform inside the panel. Modal sheets and the embedded Settings screen go away. Settings becomes a normal window.

02

Scopes, not screens

Features are filters shown as a token in the search field. Backspace on an empty field returns to Everything. Escape always goes back exactly one level.

03

Say what Return does

The action bar always names the primary action of the selection, plus ⌘↩ and ⌘K when they apply. Nothing runs that wasn't labelled.

04

Feedback where you look

Confirmations, errors and progress appear in the action bar or inline. Banners no longer stack above results or push the list down.

05

Private by construction

Opt-in moments say exactly what is stored and where. The rest of the time a single lock glyph covers it, with details one hover away.

03 · Foundations

Color, type, space, material, motion

The tokens replace the two values in RayStyle and the per-view literals: font sizes, opacities like .primary.opacity(0.075), and the hard-coded panel background. Each token has a light and a dark value, and views never use raw colors.

Accent · Ray

The current coral, rebuilt as a ten-step ramp. Light mode uses 700 for text and glyphs (6.3 : 1 on the panel) and 600 for fills behind white text (4.9 : 1). Dark mode uses 400 for text (6.2 : 1) and 500 for fills. Use the accent for focus, the caret, primary buttons and active scope tokens. It never marks the selected row; selection is neutral.

Replaces the NSMenu

The panel is a child NSPanel that becomes key while it's open. Arrow keys, Return and typing go to it, which keeps the fix for finding 1 of the September review without falling back to a plain menu.

  1. Type to filter. Every action is searchable, and its shortcut is shown so the panel teaches you to skip it next time.
  2. Item actions come first, then organisation (pin, favorite, delete), then panel-level actions. Settings and Back to Everything are no longer in this panel, because ⌘, and Esc already do them.
  3. Destructive items are red and end with an ellipsis. Choosing one closes the panel and moves the confirmation into the action bar.

Each flow shows the redesigned panel next to notes that explain the decisions. The numbered pins match the notes; use Clean in the top bar to hide them. Icons and content are illustrative.

05 · Flows, continued

AI that works on what you're doing

Today AI is a separate screen: you paste text in and copy the answer out. In the redesign, AI starts from wherever you are (a query, a clipboard entry or selected text) and sends its result back to the app you came from with ⌘↩. It still runs only on this Mac and never saves a chat unless you ask.

How do I explain the difference between structured and unstructured concurrency to a new teammate?

Explain it by asking who owns the work.

  1. Structured: child tasks live inside a scope (async let, task groups). The parent waits for them, and cancelling the parent cancels them.
  2. Unstructured: Task { } starts work nobody automatically waits for. You keep the handle and are responsible for cancelling it.

A good analogy: structured tasks are meetings on your calendar that end when the day ends. Unstructured tasks are errands you have to remember to stop.

08 · Accessibility & content

Readable, operable, plain-spoken

These are the rules behind every component, and they turn the September review findings into rules that prevent them from coming back.

Contrast is a token property

text-2 stays at or above 4.5 : 1 on both panel materials, measured over pure white and pure black wallpapers. text-3 never carries information. Increase Contrast raises separators to 16% and fills by 4 points.

11 pt is the floor

No 9 or 10 pt text anywhere, including keycaps, badges and metadata. Uppercase monospaced labels such as “LOCAL & PRIVATE” go away.

Never color alone

Diffs use strikethrough and underline as well as red and green. Status dots always come with a word. The favorite star sits next to the text “Favorite” in VoiceOver.

VoiceOver reads intent

A row reads as its label (title, kind, location), its value (subtitle) and a hint (primary action). The scope token reads “Scope: Clipboard History. Delete to leave.” Toasts are posted as announcements.

Everything by keyboard

Every action is reachable from ⌘K, and every ⌘K item shows its shortcut. Nothing depends on hover: the privacy tooltip text also appears in Help and in VoiceOver.

Motion and transparency respect settings

Reduce Motion turns springs into 120 ms crossfades. Reduce Transparency makes every material solid. The caret stops blinking when Reduce Motion is on.

Voice

Plain, specific and calm. Button labels are verbs, and the target app is named. Titles and commands use Title Case, as in macOS menus; everything else uses sentence case. Say “on this Mac”, not “local”. Errors say what happened and what to do next.

09 · Build plan

From this document to the app

Six phases. Each one ships on its own, keeps ./scripts/verify.sh green, and changes what you see before changing how it behaves. That way regressions in the September fixes (Actions routing, Escape handling, appearance) are caught at the layer where they could reappear.
